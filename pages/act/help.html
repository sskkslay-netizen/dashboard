A QUICK GUIDE TO YOUR SPACE

A few helpful notes.

Find answers about saving progress, practice tools, skill scores, and your ACT archive.

Where is my ACT data saved?

Your ACT activity is saved in this browser under studyArchiveACT, separate from the main Study Archive data. It stays after refreshes on this browser. Download a backup from Settings if you want a separate copy.

How do skill levels work?

Skills use a 0–100 score: Core is 0–39, Advanced is 40–84, and Mastery is 85–100. A quiz or lesson updates the score. You can manually set Core, Advanced, Mastery, or Paused in Settings, or return to Automatic.

How do ACT activities earn crystals?

Logging study time, completing a lesson, submitting a quiz, or finishing an in-app practice test adds crystals to your main Study Archive wallet. A logged session earns 5 plus 1 per 10 minutes (up to 35); a lesson earns 15, with 10 extra for a perfect check; a quiz earns 10 plus 5 per correct answer and 25 extra for a perfect score; an in-app test earns 20 plus 2 per correct answer. Each activity can be rewarded only once; manually entered outside-test scores do not earn crystals.

Where can I learn about an individual skill?

Open Lessons and choose any skill to read its explanation, a worked example, and a useful check. Skills with practice questions also use those questions for their lesson check.

Are quiz and practice-test questions official ACT questions?

No. Questions in this study center are original ACT-style practice. Practice-test scaled scores are rough estimates for these short practice sets, not official score conversions.

How do I keep track of a question I missed?

Save it in Incorrect Answers with your answer, the correct answer, and an explanation. You can filter the notebook, mark questions reviewed, and practice them again.

Why does the dashboard include 18 completed lessons?

The ACT center starts with the lesson history you provided as sample data. New lessons and quizzes add to that history and update your progress.

Does ACT study time change my main dashboard?

No. ACT time is tracked separately in the ACT archive, so it is not added to or double-counted with your main Study Archive focus time.
